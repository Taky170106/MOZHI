How to record a set that actually works
  1. Interleave the classes. A round of ~40 for every class, then go round again, four or five times. Recording 200 of one class and then 200 of the next lets the light, your position and your posture drift with the label — and the model learns the drift instead of the sign. This matters more than everything else here.
  2. Add a "Background" class. Hands down, standing still, walking in and out, hands moving between positions. 300+ images. Without it a five-class softmax has to call an empty counter something.
  3. Move between bursts. Step closer, further, left, right, turn a little. The crop normalises position and scale, so this costs you nothing and buys robustness.
  4. 200–300 images per sign. A few dozen is not enough to separate five signs that share a body, a face and a room.
  5. Watch the right-hand square. If your hands leave it, the sample is worthless — it says "no person found" when there is nothing to record.
Then: download a zip per class, unzip, and in Teachable Machine use Upload (not the webcam) to add each folder as its class. Train, export TensorFlow.js, drop the three files into app/public/models/tm/, and open the Counter with ?framing=1.
